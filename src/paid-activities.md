# Paid Activities

If you want to do any kind of paid activity (teaching labs, exercises, tutorship, work done for companies, etc.), it
must be approved by the PhD board. To obtain the approval:

1. download the authorization PDF form from the [PhD School website](https://www.unimib.it/didattica/offerta-formativa/dottorato-ricerca/carriera/modulistica-dottorandi), fill it in and sign it;
2. fill in the [dedicated form](https://forms.gle/cQEN58ZxGxdocdS57) and upload the signed PDF;
3. wait for the PDF signed by the PhD director;
4. send the PDF signed by the PhD director to the PhD office by email (dottorati@unimib.it).

# Paid Activities

If you want to do any kind of paid activity (teaching labs, exercises, tutorship, work done for companies, etc.) you must be approved by the PhD board. You can obtain the approval via the [dedicated form](https://forms.gle/cQEN58ZxGxdocdS57), then send the signed (by the PhD director) form to the PhD office  by email (dottorati@unimib.it)

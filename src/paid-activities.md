# Paid Activities

If you want to do any kind of paid activity (teaching labs, exercises, tutorship, work done for companies, etc.), it must be approved by the PhD board. You can obtain the approval via the [dedicated form](https://forms.gle/cQEN58ZxGxdocdS57), then send the form, signed by the PhD director, to the PhD office by email (dottorati@unimib.it).

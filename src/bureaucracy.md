# Bureaucracy

Here we describe some specific rules that apply only to PhD students in Computer Science. In a separate website you can find the [University information and rules](https://en.unimib.it/education/doctoral-research-phd-programmes/during-your-phd). You must read carefully the entire content of that section, and especially the Handbook. Keep in mind that the coordinator and the board of the PhD program in Computer Science cannot deviate from those rules.

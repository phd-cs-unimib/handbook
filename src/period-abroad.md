# Period abroad

During your PhD studies, you have to spend a research period in a foreign universities or research institutes for **at least 6 month**s. You can split this duration over different stays, also in different institutions. Students with industrial positions (alto apprendistato) might be exempted or can have their requirement decreased. Students hired on specific programs might have different rules. Your period abroad must be approved by the PhD board;the approval must be asked via the [dedicated form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScdYn0gYIefSvQPVsrpnR-NQY_NU8OFoIdjHx4rWcQQRdthyA/viewform?usp=header).

Planning for your period abroad takes somes time. You and your supervisor must agree on the host institution, the hosting researcher, the time and scope of the period abroad. Every aspect must also be agreed with the host researcher, including the intellectual property aspects.

Once your period abroad has been approved by the director, you have to send it to the PhD office. This step is essential to receive the 50% increase of your fellowship.

- If you want to apply for the **Erasmus Traineeship** program, you have to request the authorization for your period abroad at **least 4 weeks** **before** the deadline of the Erasmus Traineeship call.

# Courses

You must obtain at least **8 ECTS** from disciplinary courses and **3 ECTS** from interdisciplinary courses. Each disciplinary course consists of 2 ECTS. The first year courses are at the beginning of the academic year (November-March). We expect each student to obtain at least 6 ECTS by April of the first year, so that they can dedicate almost entirely to research after that.

We want to monitor your progress in completing the coursework, not to evaluate or control your work, but rather to identify potential difficulties as early as possible and provide support when needed. For this reason, you have to fill in the following [PhD Student Progress form](https://forms.gle/isWWu97ukkRbJzqQA) every 3 months (end of March, June, September, December).

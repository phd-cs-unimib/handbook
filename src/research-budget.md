# Research budget

Each student has a research budget available that is approximately €1,620 per year. The PhD program in Computer Science allows for flexible spending, that is, the students can spend their budget of €4,860 along the three years with no specific constraint.

The research budget is mainly meant to cover expenses during study missions or participation in conferences, seminars, and schools. Other kinds of expenses, such as article processing charges or hardware, must be discussed with the PhD director, since they can have special rules. The goal is to guarantee that the research budget is used to benefit the PhD student: any legitimate use will be allowed.

The research budget is available to all PhD students enrolled at UNIMIB, irrespective of whether they have a fellowship and of the institution providing the fellowship. The research budget is not available to PhD students enrolled in PhD programs at other universities who have a co-advisor from the Department of Informatics, Systems and Communication of the University of Milano-Bicocca.

Usually all uses of the research budget have to be approved by the PhD director. There are some special cases where the expenses must be approved by the department director or PI of a project. Students who are in this situation will be directly informed by the PhD director.

# Research budget

Each student has a research budget available that is approximately to 1,620€ per year. The Ph.D. program in computer science allows for flexible spending, that is, the students can spend their budget of €4,860 along the three years with no specific constraint.

The research budget is mainly meant to cover expenses during study missions or participation in conferences, seminars, schools. Other kinds of expenses, such as article processing charges or hardware must be discussed with the coordinator, since they can have special rules. The goal is to guarantee that the research budget is used to benefit the PhD student, any legitimate use will be allowed.

The research budget is available to all ph.d. students enrolled at UNIMIB, irrespective of the fact that they have a fellowship and the institution providing the followship. The research budget is not available to ph.d. students being part of ph.d. programs running at other universities with a co-advisor from the department of informatics, systems and communication of the University of Milano – Bicocca.

Usually all uses of the research budget has to be approved by the coordinator. There are some special cases where the expenses must be approved by the department director or PI of a project. Students who are in this situation will be directly informed by the PhD director.

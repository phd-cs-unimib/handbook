# Choosing your supervisor

Soon after the beginning of the PhD each student must choose their supervisor(s) who will be in charge of supervising the professional and scientific growth of the student. The choice of the supervisor has to be approved by the PhD board.

We expect each PhD student to continuously update the supervisor on the progress of the PhD studies, including deciding the teaching plan, and all other activities.

A supervisor can be:

1.  a professor (full, associate, or assistant) professor of the University of Milano – Bicocca
2.  a professor of another university, a researcher in a research institute or in a R&D lab of a company.

In the first case, you might have one or two co-supervisors, without any restriction on his/her affiliation. In the second case, you **must** also have a supervisor that is a professor at the University of Milano – Bicocca.

The choice of the Supervisor and the co-supervisors must be communicated using the [dedicated form](https://forms.gle/Lp5h4cxN4rRiEj4p8).

You can change your (co)supervisor choice by using the same form. All changes must be approved by the PhD board to take effect.

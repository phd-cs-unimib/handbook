# Choosing your supervisor

Soon after the beginning of the PhD each student must choose their supervisor(s) who will be in charge of
supervising the professional and scientific growth of the student.
The choice of the supervisor has to be approved by the PhD board.

We expect each PhD student to continuously update the supervisor on the progress of the PhD studies, including
deciding the teaching plan, and all other activities.

The supervisor **must** be a professor (full, associate, or tenure-track assistant) of Computer Science at the
University of Milano-Bicocca.

You might also have one or two co-supervisors, without any restriction on their affiliation (for example, a
professor of another university, or a researcher in a research institute or in an R&D lab of a company), provided
they have the scientific background needed to supervise the planned research.

The choice of the supervisor and the co-supervisors must be communicated using the [dedicated
form](https://forms.gle/Lp5h4cxN4rRiEj4p8).

You can change your (co)supervisor choice by using the same form. All changes must be approved by the PhD board to
take effect.
